What is Remote Online Notarization?

Remote Online Notarization (RON) is a modern alternative to traditional in-person notarization. Using secure video conferencing and digital technology, we can authenticate and notarize your documents from anywhere.

Whether you need to notarize powers of attorney, affidavits, loan documents, or other legal paperwork, NotaVault makes the process simple, fast, and secure.
