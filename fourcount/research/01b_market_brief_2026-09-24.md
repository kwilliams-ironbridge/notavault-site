## 2. Mechanism

Slow breathing near 6 bpm drives respiratory sinus arrhythmia, stimulates the baroreflex, raises vagal activity (Zaccaro 2018, P, https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/human-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnhum.2018.00353/full). The physiological sigh offloads CO2 and lowers arousal within a few breaths. Chronic resting-HRV change over 4 weeks is not reliable (Balban 2023 found none). Build claims on breathing rate and self-report.

## 7. Regulatory

FDA general wellness guidance, Jan 2026: stress and relaxation apps with no disease claim are not devices; may display HRV for wellness. FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance: benefit claims need RCT-grade substantiation. Apple (2026): declare medical device status in App Store Connect. Google Play: health apps declaration. Safe: trains, practice, supports, "in a controlled trial." Unsafe: treats, cures, reduces anxiety, clinically proven, diagnose, endorsement.
