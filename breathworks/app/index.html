Lesson 1 of 1

Breathe into the belly, not the chest.

Put one hand on your chest and one on your stomach. Breathe in through your nose for four seconds. The lower hand should rise first. The upper hand stays almost still.

Do that five times. This is the mechanic every technique in the program is built on.
